Your client: a running-shoe retailer

Choose a company name for your fictional UK online running-shoe shop. Its average order value is £95. Your job is to increase profitable online shoe purchases using a simulated Search advertising campaign.

No Google Ads account, real advertising or payment is involved. Results are synthetic and simplified, not predictions of Google Ads performance.
